A game like PUBG, free in your browser

The battle royale you know (the plane, the parachute, the scramble for loot, the closing circle), in a browser tab. No download and no install: pick solo, duo or squad and find a match.

What's the same as PUBG

  • The drop: a plane crosses the map. Jump when you like, open your canopy and steer to where you want to land.
  • The loot: guns, armour, helmets, backpacks, heals and grenades, each coloured by rarity so you know what you're looking at.
  • The circle: the gas closes in phases. Inside is safe; outside costs health.
  • Teams: solos, duos and squads of four, with revives.
  • Vehicles: jeeps on the roads.

What's different

  • Up to 30 players a match, not 100, on three maps cut from ZONA's world (Desert, Green West and Kamenka), so a match is quicker.
  • Six slots instead of a full backpack grid, and ammo goes straight into your pockets: no magazines to fill.
  • A lobby you fight in: wait in a small fenced area with guns to fight over, and come straight back when you fall. The match starts when two thirds of the lobby vote to go.
  • It runs in your browser on a gaming PC or laptop, and on phones in landscape.

Questions

Is it like PUBG?

It plays the same way, in your browser, with up to 30 players a match rather than 100.

Can I play with friends?

Yes: solos, duos and squads of four, with revives. You can host a lobby of your own and share its code.

Is it free?

Yes, free in your browser, with nothing to download.
