The road over, and a fall is a fall

The hill between the old valley and Kamenka is cut down along the road, so the road can be driven. There is a second injector. And nobody dies of a fall they did not take.

  • The road to Kamenka can be driven. It climbs 23 in a hundred from the floor of the old valley to the town, through a cutting with a wood on its sides. It was a wall: 105 in a hundred at the worst of it.
  • Adrenaline Injector. The injector with a yellow barrel: your stamina is full, and for ten seconds nothing you do costs any.
  • An injector is taken into the hand, then used. Its key brings it out; a click uses it; the wheel puts it away.
  • The mouse wheel breaks off what you are doing: a meal, a drink, a dressing, an injection, a reload. Nothing is lost by it. On a phone, the weapon button.
  • A helicopter's cargo is four or five things of the kind kept under the gas.
  • The bunker's door is heard by everybody on the map, from where it is.
  • The brick houses and the plank houses are drawn with their pictures at the size they were made.
  • Players died of falls they had not taken (it was reported at doorways). A fall is now measured by how far you really came down, and says so when it kills.
  • A helicopter came down with no cargo beside it.
  • A thing used out of an open crate, and broken off, was lost.
10 Oct 2026

The map grows: Kamenka

The ground is 1536 m across where it was 1024, with the old map as it was in the middle of it. The road is carried on out of the valley, over a pass, to a second town in a valley of its own. And the rules for dog tags have changed.

  • Dog tags are earned inside the Zona, the dotted line on the map. Carry a tag you took over that line and a count of fifteen seconds starts on the screen: still outside at the end of it, the tag is void. Your own tag is yours wherever you go. Kamenka and the road to it are inside the line.
  • Nobody has a tag in their first five minutes alive.
  • The country outside the line is for stashes: mountains with a wood on them all round the new edge, a squatters' house far to the south, a logging camp far to the north.
  • Nobody starts by the bunker, nor between the valley and Kamenka: 17 of the 24 starts are left.
  • A field radio calls a supply drop to where you stand, once in five minutes.
  • Hats under the helmet (a patrol cap, a boonie hat: a tenth off a hit to the head), work gloves, and three packs of 16, 30 and 42 slots.
  • The jeep rides heavier: softer springs, damped as a heavy car's are.
  • A quarter more of the common things on the old map, for the buildings added to it.
  • You could walk or drive off the edge of the ground: there are walls there that cannot be seen.
  • The heavy armour was drawn as its straps and pads with no body to it.
  • Vests stood a hand's breadth off the back and over the shoulders: each is fitted to the body.

Reloads you can see, thinner gas, more guns

A second pass with nothing new in it, and three changes to how the Zone plays: the gas is a quarter thinner, there are half as many infected again in it, and pistols and rifles are easier to find.

  • More guns to find. 24 rifles and 26 of each pistol on the open map where there were 16, with the rounds to go with them, and all three likelier in an ordinary crate.
  • Nine infected keep to the Chemical Works where six did.
  • A bed can be jumped on.
  • Hands stay on the gun. Seen from outside, a pistol is held in both fists crouched, at a run, in the air and looking down, not only standing still.
  • Nothing changes in one frame. Other players lower a gun and raise the next, and let go of it to eat, dress a wound, wave or sit, where their arms used to jump from one pose to the other.
  • After using an item the gun came back into the picture in one frame: it comes up from below.
  • Other players stuttered at a run: fifteen position reports a second were going out as thirteen.
  • Pouring a jerrycan into a jeep was drawn as drinking from it.
  • What is left on a water barrel lay 3 cm over its lid: it is on the lid.
  • Seen from outside, a rifle reload and a shotgun reload showed no movement of the hands at all.

Hands, arms and beds

No new features: a pass over how things are held and where they lie. Other players now hold a pistol round its grip, keep their arms out of their own chests, and find what they are looking for on a mattress instead of in mid-air.

  • Attachments straight off the ground. Drag a part from what lies nearby onto a gun and it is fitted: it no longer has to go in a pocket first.
  • Pistol iron sights are centred again on the M9 and the Pistol 43.
  • A sprint is kept through a jump.
  • The red arc that shows where a hit came from pointed the wrong way for shots from in front and behind.
  • Far-off trees were bare: their leaves had not been drawn at a distance since night was added.
  • On a slow frame the gun in your hands could leave the picture for a moment.
  • The changelog's pictures did not load from the address without its last stroke, which is how it is usually typed on a phone.

The bunker opens up

Bunker 17 was a corridor with a dead-end room behind every door. It is now a place people lived in, with ways through from room to room, so whoever is filling a pack in the armoury has a second door at their back.

  • Ways from room to room. Seven new doorways between neighbouring rooms. Most rooms can now be come at from behind, the armoury included.
  • A kitchen beside the mess, with a way through between them.
  • Dressed rooms. Mattresses on the beds, tables and chairs out on the floors, shelves in the middle of Records, a bench in the wash room, a rule on the wall of every room.
  • A past. Painted lines down the main passage, warning paint at its two doors, old blood, and writing on the walls.
  • Empty hands are 20% faster at every pace, crouched and walking too, from the moment you press holster.
  • Aiming is 20% slower than moving without it. It used to drop you to a creep.
  • Holographic sight ported to the M9 and the Pistol 43, centred on the slide. The Desert Eagle sits properly in the hand. The shotgun's suppressor no longer shows through.
  • Sound. The sniper's bolt is heard again. A fist landing, a heavier blow, and a round on steel are recordings.
  • Main menu. A Discord button, the latest posts from X, this changelog, the film of the squad's map, and trees in full leaf in the menu's scenes.
  • More of the infected at the bunker, above it and in it, and the bunker has gas of its own: bring a mask.
  • No ball of white light on a player carrying a lamp. The infected stand flat on their feet. Flickering faces on roofs, eaves and rails are gone. Phones have a Light button.
  • The door to Level 2 says why it is shut: it needs a Level 2 keycard, and that is still to come.

Lights out: night, lamps and Bunker 17

The Zone has a night now, and something under it. A keycard found in the gas opens a door across the map, and behind it is one dark level with the best loot in the game.

  • Day and night, kept by the server so everybody sees the same hour.
  • Lamps. A flashlight you carry (L), a light that clips under a barrel, jeep headlights, street lamps.
  • Bunker 17. A stair down, a door a keycard opens, a dozen rooms laid out by rule, lockers and racks to search, and the infected shut in with it, in orange suits.
  • The keycard lies in the gas at the Chemical Works. You cannot drive with one: it is carried to the bunker on foot.
  • New guns and parts. Desert Eagle, Benelli M3, a holographic sight, a weapon light, a suppressor for the sniper, longer magazines, a flask.
  • A hurried crouch on Shift, and nobody shoots on the run any more: the trigger stops the sprint.
  • A burned jeep is back in three minutes. A door is heard from where it is, not across the whole map.
9 Oct 2026

The Chemical Works and the gas

The map grew for the first time: a works in a cirque behind the checkpoint, sunk in gas. A mask lets you in, and what lies under the gas is worth going in for.

  • The gas zone. Without a mask it kills. Masks are found at the police station.
  • The richest loot on the map is under the gas. A scope comes in every supply drop.
  • Campfires. Stone rings can be lit. A fire mends you and warms a meal, and its smoke gives you away.
  • Broken legs. A hard landing or a round in the leg leaves you limping until you splint it.
  • A new main menu: scenes from the Zone playing behind it and the game's own map, with a layout that fits a phone.
  • A combat helmet in place of the cap, and the rifle held with a hand on the fore-end.
8 Oct 2026

The infected

The people who lived here never left. They see you, they hear you, they remember where you were, and a gunshot brings more of them than you have rounds for.

  • Sight and hearing. They see in front of them and hear footsteps, sprints and shots, each from its own distance. A suppressed 9 mm does not bring the street.
  • They live somewhere. The village most of all, the checkpoint, and a few about every outlying place. Killed, another comes when nobody is looking.
  • They use doors, beat on shut ones, and can be run down with a jeep.
  • Melee redone: a guard, blows that land when they look like they land, and a bat carried over the shoulder.
  • Every building dressed: chimneys, porches, doorsteps, eaves and shutters.
8 Oct 2026

Guns, a jeep and automatic payouts

Tags cashed in are paid in SOL by the machine, with a receipt on the chain for every payment. And the game got its guns from real weapon packs, a jeep to cross the map in, and a soldier's body to do it wearing.

  • Automatic payouts. A tag held ten minutes and cashed in is paid to your wallet without anybody pressing a button.
  • Three guns from weapon packs: the sniper, the M9 and the Pistol 43, held in the packs' own hands, each handling its own way.
  • Leaning with Q and E, seen by other players and able to be shot.
  • The jeep. Your own tag hangs round your neck, and taken tags have a pouch.
  • Recorded gunfire and a muzzle flash made from two packs.
  • Fifteen more buildings and two new places: Sosnovka and the Motor Pool.
  • Phones: every key has a button, and a weapon can be drawn, swapped and fired by touch.
  • Health, food and water are bars with numbers. Things on the ground can be eaten, drunk and opened where they lie.

Ten minutes, a wheel and fuel drums

A tag is held for ten minutes, not thirty. There is a wheel of things to shout, and red drums that go up when shot.

  • The hold is ten minutes, down from thirty.
  • A wheel of calls and emotes on T, heard by whoever is near.
  • Exploding fuel drums.
  • The X account and the contract address on the main menu.
  • An echo that grew with every shot until the game sounded like a cave.
  • Floors that swallowed steps and doors that held people in their frames.
6 Oct 2026

Supply drops, grenades and a new survivor

A crate comes down under smoke that everybody can see, and everybody wants it. Plus grenades, a minimap, a leaderboard and a new body for the player.

  • Supply drops and a leaderboard.
  • Grenades, a use for every item, a minimap, and a list of payouts.
  • A new player character: a dressed survivor in place of the stock soldier.
  • Places to arm up near every start, and weapons everywhere.
  • A line for a full server: you wait your turn instead of being turned away.
  • From testers: pings when a tag is taken, faster respawns, pick up and drop, fists.
  • Other players' shots no longer land on the shooter. Hills and doorways no longer drag. The woods cost less to draw.
  • 5 Oct: a performance and polish pass, and rewards counted in SOL.
1 Oct 2026

First playable

A survival shooter in a browser tab: drop in with nothing, loot, fight other players, take a dog tag and stay alive long enough to cash it in.

  • Multiplayer PvP on one server, with a loot economy, clothing and attachments.
  • Dog tags and the cash-in.
  • Chat, global and by distance.
  • Graphics options and the aerial main menu with a stamped dog tag.
  • Phones (2 Oct): touch controls and a landscape layout.
