Three ways to play

ZONA: survival

An open-world PvP survival game in the spirit of DayZ. Loot, fight, trust no one, stay alive.

Battle Royale

Up to 30 players, with solos, duos and squads. A plane, a parachute, a closing circle and one winner.

Arena

Fast rounds with set loadouts on a tight map. Pure aim.

A real shooter, not a toy

  • Gunplay: recoil, leaning, hit zones, armour, scopes up to 12x, suppressors and attachments.
  • Dozens of guns: pistols, SMGs, shotguns, AKs, AR-15s, and snipers from the Mosin to the Barrett .50.
  • Vehicles: jeeps on every road, helicopters to fly, supply drops to fight over.
  • A big world: coastal towns, an old heartland of villages, desert canyons, a gas zone, a sealed bunker and a mountain fort.
  • Smooth: built to run at high frame rates in the browser; see the measured numbers on the changelog.

Fair play

A bot check at the door, server-side checks on what players claim, and bans for cheat scripts keep the fights fair.

Questions

Is ZONA really free to play?

Yes, free in your browser, with nothing to download or install.

What kind of FPS is it?

A first-person PvP shooter with three modes: open-world survival, Battle Royale and Arena.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Battle Royale has duos and squads, and in ZONA you can team up with other players.

Does it run on a laptop or phone?

In a modern browser such as Chrome or Edge. A gaming PC or laptop gives the best frame rate; phones and tablets can play in landscape.
