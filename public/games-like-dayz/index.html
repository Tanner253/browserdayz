What makes it feel like DayZ

  • Start with almost nothing. Loot gets better the deeper you go: the outskirts, the villages, Kamenka, the gas zone, the bunker.
  • Other players are the real danger. Full PvP, voice and chat. Trade, team up or take what they carry.
  • Survival you feel: bleeding, broken legs, blood loss, hunger and thirst, campfires to cook on and warm up by.
  • A world worth crossing: the coast and its towns, an old heartland of villages, a desert of canyons and ruins, a mountain fort.
  • Vehicles: drive jeeps, fly helicopters, and run for the supply drops everybody hears coming down.
  • The infected roam the towns and guard the best places.

And where it's like Rust

You don't build bases in ZONA, but you can keep what you've found: put it in a stash case and bury it in the countryside with a shovel. It stays in the world, waiting for you to come back and dig it up. Raid crates, watch your back on the way home, and decide whether that player waving at you is friendly.

The guns

Recoil, leaning, hit zones, magazines to fill and armour that stops rounds.

Up close

Shotguns, SMGs, pistols, grenades, and melee when you're out of ammo.

Not in the mood to survive? Two quick modes

Battle Royale: drop from the plane, loot fast, be the last one standing as the circle closes (solos, duos and squads). Arena: short, sharp fights with set loadouts.

Questions

Is ZONA like DayZ?

Yes. It's a PvP survival shooter in the spirit of DayZ: start with little, scavenge, and every player could be a friend or a threat. It runs in your browser.

Does it have base building like Rust?

Not base building. You can keep loot in stash cases and bury them with a shovel; they stay in the world for you.

Is it free? Do I need to download anything?

It's free to play, and it runs in your browser: nothing to download or install.

What do I need to play?

A modern browser such as Chrome or Edge. A gaming PC or laptop gives the best frame rate; phones and tablets can play in landscape.
