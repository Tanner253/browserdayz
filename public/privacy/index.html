Privacy policy

ZONA is a free game played in your browser at zonapvp.fun. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and who else is involved. Last updated 22 October 2026.

What you give us

  • The name you play under. Other players see it over your head, in chat and on your dog tag.
  • A payout wallet address, if you give one (Settings, Account). It is a public Solana address: it is kept on your device and sent with the dog tags you cash in. Payments to it are recorded on the public Solana blockchain, which anyone can read.
  • What you say in chat and voice. Chat is shown to other players. Voice is relayed live to the players near you and is not recorded.

What the game keeps by itself

  • Your character: where you are, what you carry and your stashes, kept on the game server so that you find them again.
  • An identifier for your device and the network address you connect from, used to keep out bots and cheaters, to stop one player farming another, and to enforce bans.
  • Settings in your browser's storage: your name, keys, graphics, sound and similar choices. They stay on your device; clearing your browser's site data removes them.

We do not sell what we keep. We do not ask for your email address, your real name or a password.

Advertising and cookies

ZONA may show ads served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel hosts this site, and Render hosts the game servers; like any host, they see the network address of each visit.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, and Google AdSense its ads.
  • The Solana network carries any payment, publicly.

Children

ZONA is a shooter with violence and online chat with strangers. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep anything about them.
