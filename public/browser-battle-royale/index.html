How a match goes

  • The lobby: wait in a small fenced area with guns to fight over, and come straight back when you fall. The match starts when two thirds of the lobby vote to go.
  • The drop: a plane crosses one of three maps cut from ZONA's world. Jump when you like, open your canopy and steer.
  • Loot: six colour-coded slots, rarity on everything, ammo straight into your pockets with no magazines to fill, and armour, helmets and backpacks in their own slots.
  • The circle: the gas closes in phases. Stay inside or take damage.
  • Teams: solos, duos and squads, up to 30 in a match. Hold F for 10 seconds to revive a downed teammate.
  • Jeeps, grenades, and walls that stop bullets, decided by the server.
