Basements: the plan

A drawing to agree on, not the game. Real models for everything in them (the crates, shelves, lockers, lamps and doors already in the game); the walls and floors made as the houses' own are.
1 · THE WAY DOWN: A CELLAR DOOR OUTSIDE, STAIRS GOING DEEPER

Root cellar

Food and drink, tools, a little ammunition. The most common.

Workshop

Tools, parts for guns, a jerrycan, now and then a gun.

Hideaway

The rarest: somebody meant to wait it out here. The better loot.

2 · WHICH BUILDINGS
  • About four homes in ten get a basement: chosen by the map's own seed, the same on every machine and every restart. 42 homes on the map today (small, brick, two-storey, townhouse, cabin): about 17 basements.
  • Two or three layouts under each kind of house, as you asked: the same house can have a root cellar here and a hideaway there, each also mirrored and turned so no two read the same.
  • A storm cellar by some barns: the slanted doors in the yard, your farmhouse picture, with a smaller room under them.
3 · HOW IT PLAYS
  • Dark down there: a light, a torch on a gun, or the lamp hanging at the foot of the stairs.
  • Loot in containers: crates, shelves and lockers to search, each with the server's own loot, like the town's.
  • One way in, one way out: a place to hold, or a trap. Heard from above.
  • Safe from the under-the-ground rule, as the bunker and the fort's cellars are.
4 · THEN ATTICS, THE SAME WAY
  • A ladder up through a hatch in the top floor's ceiling, under the roof: lofts with a little loot, and a window to watch from.
When it ships: new loot places, so the loot is laid out afresh; what else that resets, I tell you before the deploy. Built on one house first, looked at in the game, then the rest.
