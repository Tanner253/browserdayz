# ZONA on itch.io: what to fill in

Upload `zona-itch.zip` (in this folder). It holds one small page that shows the live game from zonapvp.fun in its
itch.io mode (`?embed=itch`): no payout wallet, no contract address, no store (paid in SOL), no ads. itch.io takes no
crypto, and the game itself is unchanged. Every update to zonapvp.fun reaches the itch.io page by itself.

## Details

**Description** (paste as is):

> **ZONA is a free PvP survival shooter that runs in your browser. No download, no install.**
>
> Start with almost nothing on the coast, scavenge an open world for guns, armour, food and medicine, and decide what to do about the stranger on the hill. The infected roam the towns. Other players are worse.
>
> **Three ways to play**
> - **ZONA:** open-world survival in the spirit of DayZ. Loot tiers, bleeding and broken legs, hunger and thirst, a gas zone, a sealed bunker, a mountain fort, jeeps and helicopters. Your character and stashes are kept between visits.
> - **Battle Royale:** jump from the plane, steer your parachute, loot fast, be the last one standing as the circle closes. Solos, duos and squads, up to 30 a match.
> - **Arena:** team rounds at the mountain fort with set loadouts.
>
> **A real shooter:** recoil, leaning, hit zones, armour, scopes and suppressors, dozens of guns from the Mosin to the Barrett .50, and proximity voice chat.
>
> Plays best in Chrome or Edge on a gaming PC or laptop. Phones and tablets can play in landscape.
>
