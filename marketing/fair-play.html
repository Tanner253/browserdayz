Fair play

What the server asks of every game that talks to it

01 · Movement

How fast

Nobody gets anywhere faster than legs or a jeep can take them.

02 · Shots

What is between

A round does not pass through a hill to reach somebody behind it.

03 · Damage

How much

Nobody stays standing after more than a body can take.

04 · Loot

How near

A thing lying in the world is taken from beside it, not from across the map.

05 · Guns

Where from

A gun somebody carries is one the server itself put in the world.

06 · Dog tags

Five minutes

Nobody alive less than five minutes leaves a tag of their own to be cashed.
