Wager matches: 1v1 for a stake, first map the fort

A mode of its own on a server of its own. The server decides every hit and every health point, so a changed game can't win money.

HOW IT'S BUILT

  • Arena server: a new Render service. The open world is untouched, and it runs many matches at once.
  • The fort, alone: its walls rebuilt from data on that server: no loot, no infected, bounds round it.
  • Server-decided combat: the game sends where it shot. The server traces the shot through the walls to the opponent, as they stood when the shot was fired (rewound for lag), and decides hit, zone and damage.
  • Movement: moved by the player's own game, checked by the server (speed, walls, bounds).
  • Disconnect: 30 s to come back, then it's a forfeit. Every round logged for disputes.
